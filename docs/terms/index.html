Program description

The rcsd.info information line answers questions about the Redwood City School District — calendars, lunch menus, school info, and school board meetings — using publicly available data. English: (650) 482-8912. Spanish: (650) 399-7203. You can call or text either number.

rcsd.info is an independent community project operated by Primatech Paper Co LLC. It is not an official channel of the Redwood City School District. Answers are generated by AI and may contain mistakes — always confirm important details with your school or the district (rcsdk8.net).

Message frequency & rates

This is a conversational service: it only replies to questions you send. Message frequency varies with your usage. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. After you text STOP, we will send one final message confirming you are opted out.

Help & support

Reply HELP for help, or email team@rcsd.info.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
