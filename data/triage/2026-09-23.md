## Salient finding

**Unaudited Actuals (for the budget refresh).** The General Fund closed 2025-26 with
revenues of $147.48M and expenditures of $157.22M, an operating deficit of $9.73M
before an $11.0M interfund transfer. The book's revenue table counts that transfer as
"Transfers - RDA Negotiated Agreements" and totals $158.48M, so the two headline
revenue figures differ by exactly the transfer. Ending fund balance rose $1.27M, from
$17.11M to $18.38M. That is $5.63M better than the Estimated Actuals, mostly because
expenditures came in $9.54M under projection. Reserves are about 4.0% of expenditures,
against the 3% state minimum. Year-end balances in the other funds: Building (bond)
Fund 21, $39.07M; County School Facilities Fund 35, $22.54M; Capital Outlay Fund 40,
$7.57M.

**Charter oversight letters.** RCSD's letters find all three charters' budgets
sufficient for the current and next two years, but they flag the 5% reserve minimum in
the MOU for two of them:
- **Rocketship** is already below it: 4.48% at the end of 2025-26 and 4.79% budgeted
  for 2026-27. Its projections reach 5.72% and 7.03% in the following two years.
- **Connect** budgets deficits in all three projected years. Its fund balance falls from
  17% to 3.69% of expenditures by 2028-29, below the minimum.
- **KIPP Excelencia** stays well above the minimum (23% falling to 17%).

The letters also find that the LCAP Budget Overview for Parents does not match the
adopted budget for all three charters, and that Connect had not posted its LCAP on its
website as of 8/12.

**Enrollment and capacity.** On day 10, district schools held 6,199 students against a
capacity of 12,362, about 50%. The lowest utilization is at Garfield (18%), Taft (28%),
Roosevelt and McKinley (32% each); the highest is at North Star (90%) and Orion (79%).
Orion uses 25 of its 29 classrooms and needs 27 in 2027-28. The superintendent proposed
two studies with board action in December 2027 or January 2028: one on Bayside capacity
and middle-school access (implementation fall 2028-29), and one on Orion and small
schools (implementation fall 2028).
