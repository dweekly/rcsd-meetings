## Salient finding

**The KIPP renewal decision has a statutory clock.** KIPP's cover letter dates the
complete petition September 21, 2026. Ed Code 47605(b), quoted in the packet, requires
the board to grant or deny within 90 days of receipt. That deadline is December 20,
2026, extendable by 30 days if both parties agree. The 10/06 Schedule of Board Agenda
Items lists the 10/14 public hearing but no decision date. The December 9 and 16
meetings are the last ones before the deadline.

The petition says KIPP Excelencia is in CDE's "middle performing" renewal category and
asks for a five-year term, July 1, 2027 through June 30, 2032. Read alongside the 9/23
oversight letter: KIPP projects deficits in every year through 2028-29, but its fund
balance stays at 17% or more of expenditures, well above the 5% minimum in its MOU.

**The Orion timeline slipped a year.** The Parking Lot of Ideas changed between the
July 16 revision (9/23 packet) and the September 28 revision (this packet). The "Orion
Squeeze" (two more classrooms needed) moved from "2026-27 input; 2027-2028
implementation" to "2027-28 input; 2028-2029 implementation". "Supporting MIT &
Enrollment Plan" moved implementation to 2028-29 as well. Both changes match the study
timelines in the 9/23 Enrollment & Capacity Review.
