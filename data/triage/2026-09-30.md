## Salient finding

The deck restates LCAP Goal 3 with district-wide percentages headed "i-Ready Reading
Growth" and "i-Ready Math Growth". The slide does not say which growth measure the
percentages count. The site's growth-rebuild item has to pin that down before using
them. Reading moved from 54% (spring 2024 baseline) to 57% (spring 2025) to 60.7%
(winter 2025-26), against a 2026-27 goal of 66%. Math went from 46% to 47% to 56.4%,
against a goal of 58%.

The deck also carries *preliminary* 2025-26 CAASPP results as of 6/5/2026, labelled
"99% release" for ELA and "97% release" for math: 50.6% of students met or exceeded the
standard in ELA and 43.7% in math. English learners were at 8.1% (ELA) and 2.6% (math),
against the LCAP targets of 20% and 16%. These are district-reported early numbers, not
CDE's certified release. Do not put them on the site as final CAASPP figures.
