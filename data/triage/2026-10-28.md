## Salient findings

**Enrollment rose this year, against the budget's assumption.** The district's preliminary
Census Day count is 6,399, up 90 from 2025-26. The adopted budget's multi-year projection
assumed 6,239 and continued decline to 6,152 by 2028-29. About two-thirds of the rise is TK
(350 → 412), but K-8 also rose slightly (5,959 → 5,987). The count is uncertified; the
First Interim (December 16 on the Schedule) is where it reaches the budget.

**The KIPP renewal decision is still not on the Schedule.** The 10/22 revision lists no
grant-or-deny item for KIPP Excelencia's renewal petition. The statutory deadline from
`2026-10-14.md` still stands: December 20, 2026, unless both parties agree to a 30-day
extension. The meetings left before it are November 4, November 18, December 9 and
December 16.

**The charter unaudited-actuals letters moved from October 28 to November 4.** The 10/06
Schedule had the Connect, Rocketship and KIPP 2025-26 unaudited actuals review letters on
this agenda; the 10/22 revision moves all three to November 4, alongside the CAASPP /
Dashboard report. SARCs moved from January 20 to February 10, 2027.
