## Salient finding

Twenty-six policy revisions is a large batch, though not unusual for this board
— 2026-05-27 carried 36, and four other meetings since Nov 2025 carried 28 or
more. What is distinctive here is the concentration: nearly all of it is Series
4000 personnel policy, in marked-and-clean pairs.

The consequence for us is the Spanish side. Policy titles and bodies are
machine-translated from the English catalogue, so after the post-adoption scrape
`/politicas/` needs its translation pass re-run, not just the English rebuild —
otherwise 26 revised policies show current English text beside stale Spanish.
