## Salient findings

**The KIPP renewal decision is still not on the Schedule.** The 11/05 revision lists no
grant-or-deny item for KIPP Excelencia's renewal petition on December 9 or December 16. The
October 14 hearing memo anticipated board action on December 9. The statutory deadline
recorded in `2026-10-14.md` stands: December 20, 2026, unless both parties agree to a 30-day
extension, with staff findings published at least 15 days before the decision meeting.

**The classified settlement leans on reductions not yet identified.** With CSEA the district
has settled with all three units for 2026-27. The CSEA disclosure's multi-year projection
holds the reserve at the 3% minimum in 2027-28 and 2028-29 only by assuming $4,066,000 and
$1,865,000 of reductions, up from the $2,750,000 assumed for 2027-28 after the RCAA
settlement in August. Its 2026-27 beginning balance ($12,748,477) is the June estimate; the
unaudited actuals approved 9/23 closed 2025-26 at $18,377,937.

**Rocketship is asked for a fiscal stabilization plan.** The district's review of
Rocketship Redwood City's 2025-26 unaudited actuals finds a 2.13% reserve against a 5%
minimum, after the fund balance fell from $538,873 to $144,850 over two years, and requires
a written plan within 30 calendar days of November 13, 2026.
